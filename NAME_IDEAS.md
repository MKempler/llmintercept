## 💡 Naming Criteria

**What Makes a Good Name:**
- ✅ Easy to say/spell
- ✅ Memorable
- ✅ Conveys security/protection
- ✅ Professional (for B2B)
- ✅ Domain available
- ✅ Not too generic
- ✅ Works for enterprise buyers

**What to Avoid:**
- ❌ Too generic (AI Security, etc.)
- ❌ Hard to spell/pronounce
- ❌ Too playful (for enterprise)
- ❌ Confusing
- ❌ Already taken
