---

## 🎯 Key Principles

1. **Volume Matters**: Send more messages = more responses
2. **Personalization > Templates**: But templates save time
3. **Follow-up is Critical**: Most responses come on 2nd/3rd touch
4. **Value First**: Offer something valuable (free pilot, insights)
5. **Be Helpful**: Even if they don't buy, help them
