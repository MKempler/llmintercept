## 💡 Pro Tips

### LinkedIn Outreach:
- Personalize each message (mention their company/post)
- Keep it short (3-4 sentences)
- Offer value (free trial, insights)
- Follow up after 3-4 days

### Demo Best Practices:
- Show, don't tell (live demo > slides)
- Focus on their pain points
- Ask questions (what's their biggest concern?)
- End with clear next steps

### Don't:
- ❌ Wait for perfect product
- ❌ Overthink the outreach
- ❌ Give up after first "no"
- ❌ Try to sell too hard

### Do:
- ✅ Start today
- ✅ Send more messages
- ✅ Follow up consistently
- ✅ Focus on helping, not selling
